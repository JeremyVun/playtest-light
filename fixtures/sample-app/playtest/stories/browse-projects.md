# Browse projects as a member

Sign in as member@example.test with password member-pass-1. Search the
projects for "Quiet" and look at the results, then clear the search. Filter
the list to active projects and go to the second page. Open the first project
listed on that page and look at its tasks.

## Done when
- the project's own page is open, with the project's name as its heading
- the project's task list is shown
